Clinical Motivation

The Critical Challenge in Neonatal Care

Preterm infants face rapid, silent deterioration where minutes dictate neurological outcomes.

Care Continuity Breakdown

  • • Prenatal records are siloed from neonatal intensive care charts.
  • • Neonatologists lack instant fetal growth trajectory during resuscitation.
  • • >40% of maternal preeclampsia signs fail to trigger preemptive NICU staging.

Subtle Preterm Deterioration

  • • Neonatal sepsis, intraventricular hemorrhage, and RDS begin insidiously.
  • • Vital signs exhibit micro-variability changes hours before overt collapse.
  • • Conventional static monitors alert only after acute decompensation.

Alarm Fatigue & Distrust

  • • Over 85% of hospital ICU telemetry alerts are non-actionable alarms.
  • • Clinicians reject "black box" AI that outputs numbers without justification.
  • • Physicians require actionable biological explanations to take immediate action.
Project Mission: Unify maternal-fetal prenatal surveillance with continuous NICU multi-vital AI to detect deterioration hours earlier with 100% clinician transparency.

Prenatal Surveillance

Fetal Biometry, Doppler PI & Placental Biomarkers

Detecting intrauterine growth restriction (IUGR) and placental insufficiency before delivery.

Ultrasound Anthropometry

Hadlock Formula EFW

Integrates Biparietal Diameter (BPD), Head Circumference (HC), Abdominal Circumference (AC), and Femur Length (FL). Computes gestational age-specific percentiles against standard ±2 SD curves. Early identification of SGA (<10th percentile) and macrosomia.

Uteroplacental Perfusion

Uterine Artery Doppler

Measures Uterine Artery Pulsatility Index (UtA-PI) across 1st and 2nd trimesters. Detects persistent bilateral diastolic notching and abnormal vascular resistance—a primary hallmark of impaired spiral artery remodeling and early-onset preeclampsia.

Biochemical Screening

PAPP-A & PlGF Biomarkers

Normalizes maternal serum Pregnancy-Associated Plasma Protein-A and Placental Growth Factor into Multiples of the Median (MoM). Low PAPP-A (<0.4 MoM) combined with reduced PlGF identifies severe placental vascular risk weeks before onset.

Clinical Value: Fetal assessments directly feed newborn admission risk profiles, enabling preemptive steroid administration and scheduled NICU team standby.
Machine Learning Core

Quad-Model Multi-Modal Intelligence Engine

Leveraging tabular gradient boosting, spatial-temporal sequence learning, self-attention, and autoencoding.

XGBoost 35% Weight

Tabular & Statistical Features

Processes 15+ engineered rolling stats (mean, std, min, max, slope) over 60-min windows. Exceptional baseline calibration and robustness against noise.

CNN-LSTM 30% Weight

Spatio-Temporal Sequences

1D CNN layers extract localized vital waveforms (sharp decelerations, desaturations) while LSTM memory cells maintain long-range trend progression.

Transformer 20% Weight

Self-Attention Architecture

Multi-head attention maps vital co-dependencies across time ticks. Generates temporal attention weights pinpointing the critical minutes triggering risk.

Autoencoder 15% Weight

Unsupervised Anomaly Detector

Trained strictly on physiological stability. Measures MSE reconstruction error, alerting to unmodeled, rare physiological abnormalities.

Ensemble Superiority: Merging 4 distinct AI paradigms eliminates single-model blind spots and dramatically curtails false-positive alarms.
Decision Logic

Weighted Risk Fusion & Actionable Clinical Tiers

Transforming raw deep learning outputs into clinically actionable risk categories.

Calibrated Consensus Formula Risk = 0.35(XGB) + 0.30(CNN-LSTM) + 0.20(Trf) + 0.15(AE)

Fusion Rationale

  • • XGBoost (0.35) provides stable, validated baseline probabilistic scoring.
  • • CNN-LSTM (0.30) factors in dynamic vital trajectory acceleration.
  • • Transformer (0.20) factors in non-linear multi-vital co-dependencies.
  • • Autoencoder (0.15) catches rare unmodeled physiological deviations.
  • • 100% Deterministic: Zero hardcoded numbers or fake clinical values.
LOW RISK (< 0.35) Stable Baseline

Normal physiological parameters. Standard 60-second telemetry logging. No alarms triggered.

WATCH (0.35 - 0.70) Heightened Surveillance

Subtle trend divergence. Yellow dashboard indicator. Nursing check requested within 15 mins.

HIGH RISK (≥ 0.70) Bedside Intervention

Acute multi-vital collapse or anomaly threshold breach. Red WebSocket alarm pushed to physician.

Multi-model consensus ensures alarms fire only when corroborated by multiple independent algorithmic perspectives.
Explainable AI (XAI)

Tri-Modal Interpretability: Unmasking the Black Box

Clinicians do not just see an alert—they see WHAT vital, WHEN in time, and WHY the AI raised concern.

01

SHAP Feature Attribution

Feature-Level "Why"

TreeExplainer computes exact Shapley contribution values for each statistical feature. Reveals whether SpO2 minimum or HR variability drove the risk spike.

02

Autoencoder Residuals

Vital-Level Breakdown

Decomposes total reconstruction loss across individual vital channels (HR vs SpO2 vs RR vs Temp). Identifies exactly which physiological channel degraded.

03

Attention Heatmaps

Temporal Localization

Multi-head attention weights pinpoint the exact 5-minute window in the 60-minute timeline that triggered predictive concern, isolating transient precursors.

Clinical Trust Paradigm: Transparent mathematical attribution allows neonatologists to validate AI predictions against real medical pathophysiology in seconds.
